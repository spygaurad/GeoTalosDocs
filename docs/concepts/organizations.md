# Organizations, projects and roles

GeoTALOS is shared by teams. Everything you create, from datasets to maps and workflows, belongs to an **organization**, and only members of that organization can see it.

## Organizations

An organization is your team's workspace, for example a research group or a conservation programme. One GeoTALOS installation can host many organizations, and each one is kept completely separate: members of one organization never see another organization's data.

If you belong to more than one organization, you can switch between them. What you see always depends on the organization you have selected.

## Projects

Inside an organization, **projects** group related work. A project brings together the maps, datasets, annotation schemas and workflows for one study, so the whole team works from the same material.

## Roles

Every member of an organization has one of three roles.

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| **Viewer** | Look at maps, datasets, annotations and results. Cannot change anything. |
| **Member** | Everything a viewer can do, plus upload data, draw and edit annotations, run models and build workflows. |
| **Admin** | Everything a member can do, plus delete datasets, maps, models, projects and workflows, and manage who belongs to the organization and their roles. |

!!! tip
    Most people doing day to day analysis need the **Member** role. Give **Admin** to the few people who look after the organization, since admins can permanently delete data.
